02 / OUR HERITAGE

Some places stay with you.

A timeless hillside retreat, preserved through generations.

Built in the mid-19th century, Mirchoni sits at approximately 2,020 metres in Ayarpatta, surrounded by oak, deodar and walnut trees. Across more than 6,000 square metres, the grounds invite you to slow down and take in the landscape.
