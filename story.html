Rooted in Ayarpata

More than
a place to stay.

Mirchoni traces its origins to the mid-19th century, retaining the charm of a historic retreat while welcoming travellers with modern comforts. The resort stands in Ayarpata, among oak, deodar and walnut trees, at approximately 2,020 metres above sea level.

For generations, the beauty of Nainital has drawn people here to reconnect with nature and with one another. Today, the property continues that tradition of hospitality.
